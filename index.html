CHAPTER BY CHAPTER

My Reading Timeline

My relationship with reading wasn't always the same. Here are the moments that shaped it.

  1. CHAPTER 01 / CHILDHOOD

    Discovering Comics

    My reading journey started when a neighbor gave my family Spider-Man comics. At first, I was more interested in the pictures than the words. Eventually, I learned to follow the speech bubbles and understand the stories.

    THE BEGINNING!
  2. CHAPTER 02 / FIFTH GRADE

    Discovering Novels

    When I started reading Harry Potter, I discovered a different way of experiencing stories. Instead of relying on illustrations, I had to imagine the characters and scenes myself, almost like directing a movie in my head.

  3. CHAPTER 03 / GROWING UP

    Exploring Different Stories

    My interest in comics led me to explore other characters, stories, and television adaptations. I started realizing that storytelling could take many different forms.

  4. CHAPTER 04 / COVID

    My Reading Slump

    During COVID, I gradually lost interest in reading. School assignments made it feel more like a chore than something I actually wanted to do.

  5. CHAPTER 05 / HIGH SCHOOL

    Rediscovering Reading

    Eventually, I started choosing books based on my own interests. Discovering authors like Stephen King helped me remember why I enjoyed reading in the first place.

  6. CHAPTER 06 / COLLEGE

    Reading Today

    Now that I'm in college, I see reading differently. It's not just about finishing a book. It's about imagination, learning, and having the freedom to explore ideas that interest me.

THE NEXT CHAPTER

Becoming the Director of My Own Story

Reading novels taught me how to become the director of the movie playing in my head. Looking back, I think that same idea applies to my own life. Just as I learned to choose what I wanted to read, I'm learning to make my own decisions about what I study, explore, and pursue.
