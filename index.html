ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Reading was never a straight line.

When I was seven, I didn't even understand what I was supposed to do with a comic book. Years later, I was imagining entire scenes from novels. Then COVID happened, and reading started to feel like another assignment. My journey has been a mix of curiosity, frustration, and rediscovery.
