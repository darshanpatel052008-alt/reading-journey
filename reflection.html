THE NEXT CHAPTER

Becoming the Director of My Own Story

When I started reading novels, I realized something that changed how I thought about stories: I didn't need pictures to tell me what everything looked like.

“In a sense, I was the director.”

With comics, I could see the action right in front of me. With books, I had to imagine the characters, places, and scenes on my own. It felt like I was directing a movie inside my head, and nobody else had to see it exactly the way I did.

Why having a choice matters

My reading slump showed me that even something I enjoyed could become a chore when I didn't feel connected to it. Returning to the library and choosing books based on my interests helped me appreciate reading again. I didn't need to force myself to love every book. I needed to find the stories that interested me.

My next chapter

Now I think the idea of being a director applies to more than just reading. As I start college, I get to decide what I study, what I read, where I spend my time, and who I connect with. My reading journey isn't finished, and I don't expect it to end when I graduate. This is only the first chapter.
